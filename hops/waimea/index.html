Aroma in one line

Brewers describe Waimea as tangerine pine grapefruit thiol-strong. In Hoparoma's hop notes its thiol character is filed as thiol bomb. That places it among the varieties whose tropical and citrus punch depends heavily on bound thiol precursors, which only show up when the yeast releases them.

Thiol potential

3MH family (passionfruit, grapefruit) Significant
4MMP (blackcurrant, catty) High

Most of a hop's thiol aroma sits in the cone as odourless precursors bound to cysteine or glutathione; free thiols are a small fraction of the pool Gros et al. 2012 Kishimoto 2009. The tiers above summarise published precursor measurements and supplier statements for this variety. They are qualitative on purpose: Hoparoma's internal weighting is not published, and lot-to-lot variation is large.

Variety note: linalool/geraniol/oil/myrcene from hop-medians; beta/cohumulone unspecified.

Which yeast brings it out

To cash in the bound precursors, ferment with a strain rated high for β-lyase activity: Berkeley Yeast Tropics, Escarpment Labs Thiol Libre, LalBrew Diamond Lager, Omega OYL-402 Cosmic Punch, Omega OYL-404 Star Party, Omega OYL-405 Helio Gazer, SafLager SH-45, WLP077 Tropicale Yeast Blend, WLP830 German Lager. A clean Chico-type strain (US-05, WLP001) leaves most of this potential locked; London III and Conan lineage strains sit in between.

Rule of thumb only. See the yeast table for every strain's rating and the literature behind it.
