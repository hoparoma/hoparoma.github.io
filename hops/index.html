Hop varieties: oils, thiol potential, crop-year lots

Every variety in Hoparoma's library, with supplier brewing values, a qualitative rating for bound thiol precursors, and lot-level crop-year data where it is published. Varieties with enough verified data have their own page.

How to read the thiol columns

  • High Among the strongest known carriers of that thiol's precursors. A thiol-releasing yeast turns this into pronounced passionfruit or grapefruit (3MH family) or blackcurrant (4MMP).
  • Significant A meaningful contribution when the yeast can release it.
  • Low Aroma comes mainly from the oils. Thiols are not this hop's story.

Tiers summarise published precursor measurements and supplier statements Gros et al. 2012 Kishimoto 2009 Roland et al. 2016. Hoparoma's internal weighting is not published.
