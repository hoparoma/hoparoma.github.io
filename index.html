Hoparoma predicts a seven-axis hop aroma profile from your recipe, then learns your palate every time you log a tasting.

iOS · Coming to the App Store

Hoparoma is a recipe-planning and aroma-prediction tool. Brewing laws vary by country — in Japan, the Liquor Tax Act (酒税法) prohibits unlicensed production of beverages exceeding 1% ABV. Please comply with the laws of your jurisdiction.

Seven axes of hop character

Citrus, Tropical, Floral, Herbal, Spicy, Resin/Pine, and Dank/Earthy — broken out by Boil, Whirlpool, Dry Hop, and Cold Dry Hop stages.

Backed by literature

Stevens power laws (Lafontaine & Shellhammer, 2018), Arrhenius-based whirlpool decay (Forster, 2021), and BJCP / Cicerone / Siebel sensory vocabulary. Every coefficient cites its source.

A journal that learns your kettle

Save a recipe, brew it, log a tasting. Hoparoma computes the delta between predicted and perceived on every axis and pulls future forecasts toward the reality of your fermentation.

Fully offline, fully yours

No accounts, no telemetry, no network calls except Apple's purchase endpoints. Everything you create stays on your device.
