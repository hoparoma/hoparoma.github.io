Most brewing apps tell you what hops to use. Hoparoma computes what your recipe is likely to smell like before you brew it, by combining four ingredient layers into one science-honest forecast.

HOP

70 varieties · bound thiol precursors rated

Galaxy, Nelson Sauvin, Citra, Mosaic, and Riwaka rate high for 3MH-family or 4MMP precursors; Cascade and the noble hops rate low. Ratings draw on supplier data and published thiol research. Browse the hop library.

MALT

40 malts and grains · thiol precursor level rated

Hoparoma models malt's thiol contribution — the layer most apps leave out. Pale barley malt carries bound 3MH precursor in the hundreds of µg/kg; roasting destroys it; wheat, oat, and rye carry little. See the malt table.

Frequently asked

What's the science behind 4-axis prediction?

Peer-reviewed research from MDPI Fermentation (2022), Springer EFR&T (2022), DIAL UCL (2023), and PMC 9329171 (2024) establishes that barley malt holds up to 700 µg/kg of bound 3MH precursor, hops carry both free and bound thiols, and yeast's β-lyase enzyme is the gate that unlocks all of them.

Is Hoparoma for homebrewers or craft brewers?

Both. The yeast and malt libraries include mainstream homebrew packs and pro-pitch-only strains for commercial breweries. Availability badges make it clear what you can source at your scale.

How does crop year data work?

Hoparoma includes lot-level crop year data for 15 core hop varieties. Each carries real 2024, 2025, and 2026 analytical lots — switching crop year updates alpha acid, total oil, and oil composition, and the forecast on the radar chart follows. Varieties without lot data use 4-year aggregate medians.

What does the Suggestion engine recommend?

The Suggestion engine surfaces in the Bento card after you compose a recipe. It analyzes your 4-axis thiol forecast and proposes 2–4 category-diverse next-batch tweaks — for example, "swap Citra for Galaxy to boost free thiol" or "Phantasm 1.0 → 1.5 g/L to amplify." Top 1 suggestion per category, so hops don't crowd out yeast, malt, or booster ideas.

Do I need a specific iPhone for the AI features?

Flavor Forecast and the AI co-pilot run on Apple Intelligence, which needs iOS 26 on a supported iPhone. Where it isn't available, Hoparoma says so plainly instead of hiding the button — and the full 4-axis Thiol Forecast, libraries, and tasting log work on every supported device, AI or not.
