Source bibliography.

Hoparoma's prediction model is built on peer-reviewed research and lot-level data sources from major hop suppliers. This page lists the primary citations underpinning the four-axis thiol biotransformation engine.

Thiol biotransformation chemistry

Sasaki, K. et al.2008
Thiol precursor pathways in hop varietals — biosynthesis and biotransformation routes.
ISHS Acta Horticulturae · International Society for Horticultural Science
Foundational paper for Hoparoma's identification of polyfunctional thiol precursors (3MH, 3MHA, 4MMP) across hop varieties.
Sharp, D. C.2021
Quantifying 3MH expression and varietal contribution across the contemporary American IPA hop palette.
MBAA Technical Quarterly · Master Brewers Association of the Americas
Source for the per-variety thiol quantification range used in Hoparoma's tier-1 hop catalog (Citra, Cascade, Chinook, Galaxy, Vic Secret, Eclipse, Nelson Sauvin, Strata).
Brauwelt / Hopsteiner2025
LC-MS/MS chromatographic methodology for crop-year hop oil and thiol precursor analysis.
Brauwelt International · Hopsteiner Lab Research Brief
Analytical methodology source for Hoparoma's crop-year 2024 / 2025 / 2026 lot-level data integration. Cross-references the Hop Alliance and Yakima Chief lot CoAs cited below.

Yeast β-lyase activity (Pro tier classification)

Roland, A. et al.2016, 2018
Polyfunctional thiol release by Saccharomyces cerevisiae β-lyase across commercial fermentation strains.
Journal of Agricultural and Food Chemistry · Two-paper series
Source for Hoparoma's β-lyase activity threshold (≥0.85) used to classify six Pro-tier yeast strains: helio_gazer, berkeley_tropics, thiol_libre, cosmic_punch, wlp077_tropicale, star_party.
Chenot, C. et al.2019, 2021, 2023
Yeast biotransformation of bound cysteine and glutathione precursors during fermentation — quantitative analysis across strain expression profiles.
Journal of the American Society of Brewing Chemists (JASBC) · Three-paper series
Extended dataset for Hoparoma's per-strain β-lyase activity scoring — including the 2023 paper on special malt thiol precursor profiles that informs the malt biotransformation axis.
Hellwig, M. & Henle, T.2022
Kilning oxidative changes and thiol group availability across EBC color spectrum.
European Food Research and Technology · DOI:10.1007/s00217-022-04144-2
Source for Hoparoma's malt thiol precursor classification (EBC < 10 high precursor / EBC > 500 negligible). Underpins the malt-axis scoring across 38 malt types.
Molitor, A. et al.2022
Free thiol generation in beer from genetically modified yeast plus barley malt — sorghum and wheat comparison.
Fermentation (MDPI) · 8:370
Source for Hoparoma's non-barley grain precursor classification (wheat / rye / oat thiol contribution).

Lot-level analytical data sources

Hop Alliance
Lot-level Certificates of Analysis for crop years 2024 and 2025. Source for tier-1 variety alpha acid percentages, oil composition, and harvest-specific data points (e.g., Galaxy Lot 94-203-008, Citra Lot PE03316, Cascade Lot CAS25-CLS).
Yakima Chief Hops
2025 crop report and per-variety lot CoAs for proprietary American hops. Cross-reference source for crop year 2025 alpha acid, oil, and cohumulone data.
Hopsteiner
2025 lab research brief and per-variety LC-MS/MS thiol precursor analysis. Source for the analytical methodology integrated into Hoparoma's compound µg/L prediction.
BarthHaas
2024-2025 hop varietal technical sheets for European and American varieties. Source for aggregate-tier hop catalog data (Hallertau Mittelfruh, Saaz, Ekuanot ranges).
John I. Haas
Proprietary varietal hop research and 2024 USA crop report. Cross-reference source for Cascade, Citra, and Mosaic alpha acid trend data.
HPA (Hop Products Australia)
2025 Australian crop report. Source for Galaxy, Vic Secret, Eclipse, and Enigma per-lot data.
