WLP077 Tropicale Yeast Blend

A non-GMO blend of two conventional strains that White Labs found to have high β-lyase activity, offered as a homebrew-friendly alternative to engineered thiol yeasts.

What the manufacturer says

This is a blend of non-GMO yeast strains that have been carefully selected to aid in the release of bound thiol compounds by targeting high enzymatic B-lyase activity.

Product page

What the evidence says

  • White Labs screened its library for β-lyase activity and blended WLP037 Yorkshire Square with WLP830 German Lager; in a split-batch trial against WLP001, gas chromatography found 3MHA above its sensory threshold only in the WLP077 beer. White Labs, WLP077

Rating context: manufacturers usually quote free-thiol concentration relative to the sensory threshold, not conversion yield. Peer-reviewed work finds that only a small fraction of bound precursors is converted in beer fermentation, so the ordering between strains is more reliable than any absolute number Chenot et al. 2021 Michel et al. 2019.

In practice

Attenuation around 75 to 82 percent with low flocculation, so expect some haze. A good first thiol yeast for anyone who wants to avoid engineered strains.

Non-GMO (blend).

Hops that reward this strain

Citra, Cryo Citra, Cryo Mosaic, Cryo Simcoe, Ekuanot, Galaxy, Hallertau Blanc, Kohatu, Mosaic, Nelson Sauvin, Riwaka, Sabro, Simcoe, Strata, Superdelic, Talus, Vista, Waimea. Each carries a high rating for bound 3MH-family or 4MMP precursors. Mash and whirlpool additions supply precursors during fermentation; a cold dry hop mostly adds the free fraction.
